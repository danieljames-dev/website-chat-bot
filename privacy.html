Privacy

The Website Chat Bot sales pages and Standard Setup tool do not include analytics, advertising trackers, cookies, account sign-in, or a server-side form submission.

Business facts entered into Standard Setup are processed in your browser and written into the configuration file you choose to download. The setup tool does not send those facts to a server.

If checkout is activated, the purchase button sends you to Stripe-hosted checkout. Stripe processes the payment information on its own checkout surface; this static product site does not receive your card number.

The installed bot itself runs in the visitor's browser from the facts in its configuration and does not make network calls for conversations.
